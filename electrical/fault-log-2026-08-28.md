# Fault log — tube power faults (2026-08-28 → 29)

Field troubleshooting log. Author: Jessi. Main breaker **OFF** until the
re-energize protocol passes. Wiring context: [led-wiring.md](led-wiring.md),
[bus-bar-map.md](bus-bar-map.md), [../lights/controllers.md](../lights/controllers.md).

## Symptoms (first power-up after the leg split / F1 work)

1. Some tubes dark — no power.
2. A few tubes stuck full white.
3. Two tubes blue-white and **extremely hot** — power killed at the 250 A
   main disconnect.

## ROOT CAUSE — found 2026-08-29 (board with hot tube #1)

**The hot tube's ground (white wire) and the buck converter's ground — the
receiver's entire data ground reference — shared ONE doubled fork terminal
on ONE − bus-bar screw, and that joint was loose.**

When that single joint degraded:

- The tube lost its return path → supply current returned through its
  **DATA wire** (first LED chip → green 20 AWG → receiver port → WAGO →
  buck → − bar). The tube powered itself through its data pin =
  blue-white glow + extreme heat.
- The receiver's ground reference degraded at the same time → marginal
  data to **all** tubes on the board = the stuck-white neighbors.
- One bad joint explains every symptom on this board.

Status: the joint was unscrewed and re-landed during diagnosis (evidence
reseated). Buck ground re-landed; − bar ↔ receiver G screw continuity
0.00 Ω PASS. **Wiggle-test verification still owed.**

## As-built facts established (docs corrected below)

1. **Wire colors, from the factory tube label:** red = VCC +24 V,
   **white = GND**, pigtail carries (2)DATA + (3)2DATA (redundant data
   pair). Hot tube #1's wiring confirmed correct: red → + bar, white →
   − bar, both data wires in one D terminal. RGB channel order.
2. **Per-tube power** is separate red/white wires direct to the bars (some
   through 3-pin WAGOs with a 16 AWG jumper). The pigtail is **data only**;
   its power conductors are UNUSED and must be capped/taped — they are
   live when the car is on.
3. ⚠️ **No 1000 µF injection caps exist anywhere on the car**, despite
   [led-wiring.md](led-wiring.md) specifying one per tube (ordered, listed
   in the BOM — never installed). The reversed-cap theory is dead.
   **Treat the electrical docs as aspirational until verified on the car.**
   Post-repair note: the caps were designed in for good reason — expect
   flicker on white-heavy patterns until they're actually fitted.
4. ⚠️ **Receiver ground topology (all 11 boards):** receiver G wires +
   buck 12 V output(−) (blue wires) meet in a WAGO 221; the only path to
   the − bar is **through the buck** (24 V input black wire → bar). Buck
   measured non-isolated (0.00 Ω) on board #1, so it works — but the
   "bond every SRx4 ground to the − bus" requirement in
   [../lights/controllers.md](../lights/controllers.md) is **not** met by a
   dedicated bond on any board. Output ports carry D only (port G unused —
   OK, port G shares the board ground plane).
5. Buck converter (YAXIGE 24→12 V 4 A): input red(+)/black(−), output
   yellow(+12)/blue(−).
6. **Doubled fork terminals exist on every bus bar car-wide** — a design
   pattern, and the background risk behind this failure. Vibration will
   loosen more.

## Measurements — isolated tubes (Klein MM300; red clip on red wire = forward)

| Test | Good tube (baseline) | Hot tube #1 |
|---|---|---|
| Ohms | ~917 Ω both directions | ~2.5 kΩ both directions |
| Diode fwd | 0.770 | 1.030 |
| Diode rev | 0.410 | 0.585 |
| Lead resistance | 0.2 Ω (subtracted) | — |

Interpretation: hot tube #1 is not shorted and not fully open. ~3× the
baseline resistance with elevated drops both ways = partial internal
damage (some of the 96 parallel segments burned open). Expect dead
sections; final verdict needs the powered test. Best case = backup spare.

Meter notes: MM300 200 Ω range maxes at 200 (OL above); the same value
reads 2.55 on 20k / 02.5 on 200k / .002 on 2M (≈2.5 kΩ) — always record
the range. Diode + continuity share one dial position. Use alligator
clips; fingers on probe metal at high ranges fake leakage.

Also learned: **bar-to-bar (+↔−) resistance readings are meaningless
in-circuit** — the breaker only breaks +, so any bar pair sees every
tube's input circuitry in parallel (originally blamed on the caps; the
caps don't exist — readings settle faster but stay untrustworthy). Only
copper-path checks and isolated-branch checks are decisive.

## Update 2026-08-29 (later) — bus-bar landing rework

- **Still two hot tubes total** (#1 and #2); neither powered-tested yet.
- **Rework done:** doubled forks eliminated. Tubes' 22 AWG V/G wires now
  gather in WAGOs; one 16 AWG jumper per WAGO group lands on its own bar
  screw. New watch-item: each 16 AWG ground jumper is now the shared
  return for its whole WAGO group — a bad one takes the whole group's
  ground (same mechanism as the root cause, bigger blast radius). Wiggle-
  test each group jumper; keep groups ≤ ~4 tubes per 16 AWG.
- **Fuse inventory confirmed (repo-wide search):** battery-pack internals
  (50 A/module + 200 A master) and the 250 A LED main breaker are the ONLY
  protection. No per-board or per-tube fusing exists (fused distribution
  blocks were doc-listed as optional, never installed). A tube fault at
  5–10 A is invisible to all of it — hence the 3 A inline fuse rule for
  powered tests of wounded tubes.
- **No inline fuse supplies on hand** → powered tests of the hot tubes are
  DEFERRED. Both hot tubes stay disconnected (each tube has its own data
  line, so the car runs fine minus two tubes). Get an automotive inline
  blade-fuse holder + 3 A fuses before testing.
- **G→bar jumper install detail:** the receiver's power input block has
  two V and two G screws — buck's blue (12 V −) wire stays on one G screw,
  the new jumper lands on the **second, free G screw**. Bar end: prefer a
  ring lug on a **free M6 stud**; if no stud/screw is free, doubling the
  jumper's fork with the **buck's black (24 V input −) wire** is
  acceptable — both carry only milliamps-to-<1 A, and the jumper makes the
  reference redundant (two paths) instead of single-point. Never share
  with tube-ground landings. Restack flat, torque, tug test.

## Verdicts / suspect list

- **Hot tube #1:** WOUNDED, pending powered test. Never straight onto the bars.
- **Hot tube #1's original receiver port:** UNVERIFIED — the driver chip
  took the return current. Test with a known-good tube before trusting.
- **Hot tube #2:** not yet examined. Prime theory = same failure mode.
  First check: its white-wire landing — doubled screw? sharing that
  board's buck ground?
- **Dark tubes:** still presumed open joints — walk bar↔trunk continuity,
  ground bonds, per-drop continuity.
- **Stuck-white tubes on board #1:** likely collateral of the ground
  fault; re-evaluate after fix + re-energize before replacing anything.

## Fix list (every board)

1. **Dedicated 20 AWG jumper: receiver G input screw → − bus bar, own
   fork.** Stops trusting the buck's internals for the mandatory data
   ground bond.
2. Buck ground and tube grounds **never share a screw**.
3. Doubled forks: restack flat back-to-back, torque, individual tug test.
   Re-crimp any with heat discoloration.
4. Tape/cap the unused pigtail power conductors.
5. Witness-mark screws with paint pen at repair time. (Red-tape V+
   convention already satisfied — red wire = VCC from the factory.)

## Powered test protocol — hot tube #1 (not yet run)

**Blocker: power source.** Bench supply 24 V @ 1–2 A limit, or a 3 A
automotive fuse inline in the + wire if the battery must be used.

1. Tube in open air, full length visible, non-flammable surface, attended.
2. Data: move the tube's green plug to an **empty** receiver header
   (pluggable green blocks pull straight off; check that position's orange
   fuse is populated; photo the receiver first). No empty header → borrow
   a working neighbor's, leave the neighbor unplugged; do NOT seat
   anything on the suspect port.
3. The tube will show the wrong pattern slice (pixel mapping) — irrelevant.
4. Energize LEDs-dark first, watch current (~0 expected), then low
   brightness single color, several minutes, hand-check full length.
5. Any warm spot / discolored jacket → dead pile. Dead sections but cool →
   emergency spare at best.

## Re-energize protocol (whole car)

- All continuity checks pass on affected boards; wiggle-proof the
  re-landed buck-ground joint first.
- LEDs commanded dark (`python -m glorbleds off all`); watch the shunt
  ammeter; probe **+24 V** (−24 V = still reversed) at repaired pigtails;
  low brightness; touch-check repaired areas several minutes.
- 250 A sits behind 18 AWG drops with no per-drop protection: never
  first-energize repaired work from the bank unfused.
