# Project history

Origin story and per-year build notes. Sourced from the docx files preserved at [archive/org/](archive/org/).

## Origin: Project Glory to Glorb (Jan 2023 kickoff)

Glorb began as a redesign / rebuild of an existing 2022 art car platform ("Glory"). The 2023 effort was the first big push toward the current vehicle.

**Original project values:** friendship, adaptability, adventure, "no pooping." Hence the half-joking "Easily-poopable design" anti-goal and the running joke `DO NOT POOP ON THIS CAR OR ELSE` sign.

**2023 design goals:**

- Permanent shape (vs. on-playa build/teardown — that was too much in 2022)
- Floor-mounted batteries (easy access for troubleshooting, sealed against liquid)
- Big "WOWww factor" day and night — Greek palace / white curtain / gold during the day, LED at night
- "ONE BIG HAND" was on the table as a design direction
- Open sides for sightlines, ladder up to second floor

**2023 phasing schedule (from the kickoff doc):**

- Jan: solve battery crisis to get Glorb off trailer
- Feb: DMV app opens (Feb 15)
- Mar (week 2): Boise build week — install suspension, fix brakes, batteries phase 2

- Aug: "Finish/cum together"

## Major change: Tesla battery swap (May 2023)

Replaced the original 6× EG4 LifePower4 12 V 400 Ah main pack with **6 Tesla modules in 3s2p** (72 V nominal), keeping the existing EG4s as the auxiliary 12 V bank. New Tesla pack is lighter (360 lb vs. 654 lb), cheaper ($4 800 vs. $9 000), and capable of much higher continuous current. Custom BMS using EV Stealth cell-tap boards. Anthony built the new battery cage. See [electrical/batteries.md](electrical/batteries.md).

## 2023 build (Anthony, fabricator)

Fabricator scope from the build list:

- Weld and seal side compartments + middle compartment
- Flush brakes, repair/replace brake lines
- Route/tuck electrical and brake lines under the car (with care: the car gets forklifted onto the trailer)
- Aux battery storage compartment + dashboard cover (sealed)
- Main frame structure rated for 10–20 people on the 2nd floor, vibration-tolerant
- Switched the 2nd-floor staircase (in renderings) to a rear ladder
- Hinged access in floorboard for battery compartment
- Differential seals (DMV requires no leaks, anywhere)
- Fixed missing lug nut on right passenger side wheel
- Open question at the time: where the big inverter lives — side compartment, under floor, or on top of aux batteries

## 2023 improvements priorities

Originally captured pre-build:

**Priority 1:** suspension upgrade, battery placement, dashboard upgrade, brake lines, full wiring rerun, easier service access.

**Priority 2:** ventilation for battery compartment (with replaceable HVAC filters), upgraded charging center, sealed doors with high-temp dust seal + drainage, LED access pockets with gaskets, designated speaker pockets (sub centered, batteries fore/aft), tow strap / tie-down corners.
