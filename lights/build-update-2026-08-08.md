## Done

- **Flipped tubes on 3 sections.**
- **Connected 14 data lines to the receiver:**
  1. Strip data lines on female pigtails — 2× 22 AWG.
  2. Cut & strip both ends of the data extension — 20 AWG.
  3. Crimp 22–16 AWG butt plugs to connect them.
  4. Insert the free extended data line into the green terminal.
- **Connected power & ground wires on each tube bus bar (+ and −):**
  1. Strip V & G lines.
  2. Connect all 10 terminals:
     - 4 sets of 3-pin WAGO with 16 AWG & 2× 22 AWG.
     - 1 set of 2-pin WAGO — 16 AWG + 22 AWG.
     - 5 direct: crimp one side with a #8 spade terminal (22–16 AWG) & attach to the bus.
- **Successfully tested:** power, receiver, Falcon.
