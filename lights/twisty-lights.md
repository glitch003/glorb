# Twisty lights

Lights coiled around 4 vertical poles (legs).

- Coil calculator: <http://production-solution.com/coil-calculator.htm>
- Likely max wattage from Dodge Ram alternator: **160 A @ 12 V = 1 920 W**
- Strip ordered: <https://www.aliexpress.com/item/32943534376.html>
  - 14.4 W / m @ 60 LEDs/m
  - 1.2 A / m @ 12 V, 60 LEDs/m

## Pole geometry

- Pole length: **8 ft (2 438.4 mm)**
- Pole diameter: **4 in (101.6 mm)**
- Real wire diameter: 10 mm (calc adjusted up to leave space between)

## Wire-spacing scenarios

| Spacing (mm) | Turns | Length per pole (m) | 4-pole total (m) | LED count | Amps @ 12 V | Watts |

| 40 | 61 | 27.141 | 108.564 | 6 513.84 | 130.28 | 1 563.32 |
| 80 | 30 | 16.997 | 67.988 | 4 079.28 | 81.59 | 979.03 |
| 100 | 25 | 16.000 | 64.000 | 3 840.00 | 76.80 | 921.60 |

> Spacing column is set so the coil collapses to a single helical layer (number of layers = 1).
